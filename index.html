🤔 What Is desktop-fly?

desktop-fly is a delightful desktop companion that puts a tiny, realistic 3D fruit fly right on your macOS desktop. But this isn't just a pretty animation — it's a living simulation. The fly's brain is modeled after the real FlyWire connectome, meaning every movement, twitch, and pause is driven by actual spiking neural activity, just like a real fruit fly.

Think of it as a virtual pet with a scientific heart. Watch it explore your screen, react to your mouse movements, and simply exist in its own little world. It's equal parts art, science, and entertainment.

✨ Key Features

  • 🧠 Real Neuroscience Simulation — Powered by the actual FlyWire connectome data, the fly's behavior emerges from genuine spiking neural networks.
  • 🖥️ Interactive Desktop Pet — The fly walks, grooms, and explores your desktop. Move your mouse and watch it react in real-time.
  • 🎨 Stunning 3D Graphics — A beautifully rendered 3D fruit fly with realistic anatomy and smooth animations.
  • ⚡ Lightweight Performance — Optimized to run smoothly without hogging your system resources.
  • 🔬 Educational Value — Watch neuroscience in action and learn how a tiny brain drives complex behavior.
  • 🔄 Always Different — Because it's a live simulation, no two moments are ever the same. Your fly has its own personality!

🔬 How It Works

At the core of desktop-fly is a spiking neural network — a computational model that mimics how real neurons communicate. The FlyWire project mapped the complete connectome (the wiring diagram) of a fruit fly's brain, containing roughly 100,000 neurons and 50 million connections.

desktop-fly takes this real data and runs it in real-time. Each spike in the network influences the fly's virtual muscles, creating natural, unpredictable movements. It's not scripted or pre-animated — every action emerges from the simulation itself.

The result is a desktop companion that feels genuinely alive, with behaviors you'll never fully predict.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is desktop-fly safe to run?

Yes! desktop-fly is a simple desktop application that runs entirely on your machine. It doesn't access the internet, collect data, or interfere with your other applications.

Can I interact with the fly?

Absolutely. Move your mouse around and the fly will notice. It may follow your cursor, avoid it, or become curious — depending on the real-time state of its neural network.

Will it slow down my computer?

No. desktop-fly is highly optimized. It uses very little CPU and memory, so you can keep it running all day without noticing any performance impact.

Can I turn it off?

Of course. Simply quit the app like any other macOS application (right-click the dock icon and select Quit, or press Cmd+Q).
